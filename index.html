Our Purpose

A studio built on balance, breath, and intention.

At Studio Zenya, Pilates is more than exercise — it's a practice of self-discovery. We create a supportive environment where every body, regardless of starting point, can build strength, find balance, and grow in confidence through mindful movement.

✦

Small Groups

Personal attention in every session, with class sizes that let us see and support you.

✿

Certified Instructors

Trained in mat and reformer Pilates, our team adapts every class to all levels.

◐

Modern Equipment

Premium reformers and a thoughtfully designed studio space in the heart of Mkalles.

❋

Welcoming Community

From your first class to your hundredth, you'll always feel at home with us.
